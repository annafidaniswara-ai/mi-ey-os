# MI-EY Instructor Playbook

**Status: Draft — guidance not yet authored or approved.**

## Scope

Provide instructors with practical guidance for preparation, facilitation, observation, adaptation, safety, and reflection.

## Planned outputs

- Session preparation checklist.
- Facilitation and inclusive-language guidance.
- Observation without diagnosis or labeling.
- Adaptation and stop/modify guidance.
- Post-session reflection and incident-escalation procedure.

## Quality requirements

Use clear operational language, distinguish observation from interpretation, protect child privacy, and make supervision and safety responsibilities explicit.

## Dependencies

Vision, principles, framework, Movement Experience Library, and reviewed lesson template.
