# Movement Experience Library

**Status: Draft — catalog not yet populated.**

## Scope

Maintain reusable movement experiences with enough detail for instructors to prepare, adapt, deliver, and review them consistently.

## Proposed entry fields

Unique ID, title, intention, competency links, suggested age/readiness, setup, space and equipment, steps, duration, adaptations, safety considerations, observation cues, evidence/source notes, version, and review status.

## Quality requirements

Every experience needs a clear intention, accessible alternatives, practical safety guidance, and observable cues. Do not publish experiences as approved until reviewed.

## Dependencies

Framework definitions, Lesson Engine requirements, Instructor Playbook, and safety-review process.
