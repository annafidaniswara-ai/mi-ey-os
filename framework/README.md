# Developmental Competency Framework

**Status: Draft — not yet authored or approved.**

## Scope

Define movement-learning competencies and observable indicators for MI-EY OS. The first deliverable focuses on ages 2–4 while preserving the intended platform scope of ages 2–6.

## Planned outputs

- Domain map and competency definitions.
- Age-band considerations and observable indicators.
- Examples and adaptation guidance.
- Safety, inclusion, and evidence notes.
- Review rubric and version history.

## Quality requirements

Do not present age bands as universal milestones. Distinguish research evidence from instructional choices, cite credible sources, and avoid diagnostic language. Have qualified educators review the framework before operational use.

## Inputs and dependencies

Vision, design principles, architecture, research review, and Product Owner approval of the framework structure.
