## 3. Review sequence and role-specific questions

### A. Movement educator / instructor usability

- Is each competency definition understandable without specialist interpretation?
- Can the indicator be observed during the described activity?
- Does the example separate observed action from an interpretation about the participant?
- Are the proposed options practical for a real instructor to offer?
- Which terms, instructions, or fields are confusing or redundant?

### B. Early-childhood development and inclusion

- Does the wording avoid universal age deadlines or assumptions that every child follows one sequence?
- Are alternative ways to participate meaningful, not merely theoretical?
- Does the draft avoid treating communication style, refusal, pause, route choice, or need for support as failure?
- Are developmental claims appropriately limited for the evidence available?

### C. Safety reviewer

- Are the surface, equipment, spacing, supervision, and stop/modify conditions explicit enough?
- Could any suggested setup unintentionally encourage climbing, collision, throwing toward people, or movement beyond comfort?
- What safety conditions are missing for a particular activity?
- Which activities need to be removed or rewritten before any real-world use?

### D. Research / evidence reviewer

- Does each scientific claim accurately reflect the source cited?
- Is the population in the source relevant to the age range and context claimed?
- Are systematic-review limitations, uncertainty, and transferability stated?
- Which claims require a primary source, full-text check, or removal?

## 5. Critical blockers

The draft must not be moved to operational use while any material issue remains unresolved concerning:

- child safety or supervision;
- privacy or identifiable child data;
- diagnostic or stigmatizing interpretation;
- inaccurate or unsupported scientific claims;
- inaccessible participation options that materially exclude users.

A review outcome of “Pass for draft” means only that the reviewer finds the current wording suitable for the next drafting stage. It is not psychometric validation, clinical endorsement, or proof of effectiveness.

## 6. Evidence context to check

- World Health Organization. (2019). *Guidelines on physical activity, sedentary behaviour and sleep for children under 5 years of age.* https://www.who.int/publications-detail-redirect/9789241550536  
  Scope note: population-level guidance; it does not validate MI-EY competency indicators or mastery expectations.

- Wick, K., et al. (2017). *Interventions to Promote Fundamental Movement Skills in Childcare and Kindergarten: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis.* Sports Medicine. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-017-0723-1  
  Scope note: review findings and certainty limitations must be checked against the paper; this source does not validate the MI-EY framework.

Reviewers should verify the original source and only endorse claims directly supported by it. Do not infer that a general physical-activity guideline establishes a particular competency progression.
