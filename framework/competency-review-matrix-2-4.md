## 2. Review scale

For each criterion, mark:
- **P — Pass:** adequate for the current draft; rationale recorded.
- **R — Revise:** change required; record issue and proposed action.
- **N/A — Not applicable:** explain why.
- **NR — Not reviewed:** default state until an actual review occurs.

Do not pre-fill reviewer outcomes. No review should be recorded unless it was actually completed. A document commit is not evidence of a content review.

## 6. Review sequence

1. **Movement educator:** checks whether the competency wording and examples are usable and observable.
2. **Development/inclusion reviewer:** checks flexible framing, alternate participation, and the absence of universal age deadlines.
3. **Safety reviewer:** checks equipment, space, supervision, and stop/modify conditions for proposed activities.
4. **Research reviewer:** checks references and the exact claims each source supports.
5. **Product Owner:** reviews the recorded findings, approves or revises the product direction, and authorizes any status transition.
6. **Revision pass:** update competency records, increment version, and re-read changed GitHub files.
7. **Operational gate:** keep the framework Draft until critical issues are resolved and required human reviews are documented.

## 7. Evidence notes

The systematic review by Wick et al. (2017) included 30 trials involving 6,126 preschool children aged 3.3–5.5 years; the authors reported very low certainty in treatment estimates and cautioned that evidence was based on low-quality studies and immediate post-intervention effects without long-term follow-up. This is relevant background, not validation of the MI-EY framework. See https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-017-0723-1 and https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28386652/.

WHO's 2019 guidance for children under five provides population-level recommendations about physical activity, sedentary behaviour, and sleep. It does not validate individual MI-EY competency indicators or age-specific mastery expectations. See https://www.who.int/publications-detail-redirect/9789241550536.
