# MI-EY Lesson Engine

**Status: Draft — conceptual only.**

## Scope

Specify how a lesson plan could be assembled from a learning intention, competency, participant context, duration, space, equipment, and safety constraints.

## Planned outputs

- Lesson template and required fields.
- Selection and adaptation rules.
- Session structure and timing guidance.
- Safety checks and observation prompts.
- Review and acceptance criteria.

## Guardrails

Do not generate a final lesson without enough context to assess safety. Explain assumptions, offer adaptations, and keep human instructors responsible for supervision and final decisions.

## Dependencies

Developmental Competency Framework, Movement Experience Library, Instructor Playbook, and approved quality criteria.
