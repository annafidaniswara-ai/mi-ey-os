# MI-EY OS — Vision

**Status: Draft — awaiting Product Owner review.**

## Vision statement

MI-EY OS aims to provide a coherent, child-centered system for planning, delivering, observing, and improving movement-learning experiences for children ages 2–6.

The first instructional framework deliverable will focus on ages 2–4. This narrower first deliverable does not reduce the intended platform scope of ages 2–6.

## Intended users

- Educators and movement instructors.
- Program designers and curriculum developers.
- Coordinators responsible for consistency and quality.
- Families, where appropriate and with suitable privacy protections.

## Intended outcomes

1. Make movement-learning goals and experiences easier to plan consistently.
2. Connect developmental competencies to lesson design and observation.
3. Support adaptation to differences in readiness, access, context, and individual needs.
4. Create a traceable library of experiences and instructor guidance.
5. Support continuous improvement through documented feedback and review.

## Initial product boundaries

MI-EY OS is an educational planning and delivery support system. It is not a diagnostic tool, does not replace professional assessment, and should not rank children using unsupported or inappropriate norms.

## Measures to define later

Proposed measures include completeness of lesson plans, usability feedback from instructors, alignment between goals and activities, safety-review completion, and traceability of evidence. Baselines and targets remain undecided until the Product Owner approves a measurement plan.

## Open review questions
