# MI-EY OS — Design Principles

**Status: Draft — awaiting Product Owner review.**

1. **Child-centered:** prioritize children's dignity, engagement, agency, and varied developmental pathways.
2. **Developmentally responsive:** treat age bands as planning guides, not fixed descriptions of every child.
3. **Playful and purposeful:** connect movement experiences to clear learning intentions while preserving exploration and joy.
4. **Inclusive and adaptable:** plan alternatives for different abilities, environments, confidence levels, and equipment availability.
5. **Safety by design:** include space, equipment, supervision, intensity, and stop/modify criteria appropriate to each activity.
6. **Evidence-aware:** cite reliable sources for scientific claims and distinguish research findings from interpretation and design decisions.
7. **Observable, not reductive:** use specific observations to inform instruction; avoid unsupported labels, diagnoses, or simplistic rankings.
8. **Privacy-preserving:** minimize data collection and use fictional or anonymized examples during design and testing.
9. **Traceable:** link competencies, lesson intentions, activities, observation prompts, and review notes.
10. **Human-accountable:** AI-generated content remains a draft until reviewed by a responsible human.
11. **Iterative:** test small, record feedback, and revise with a documented rationale.

## Practical application

Every proposed lesson should state its intention, age/readiness considerations, setup, instructions, adaptations, safety notes, observation cues, and criteria for reviewing the experience.
