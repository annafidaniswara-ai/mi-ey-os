# MI-EY OS — Design Validation Example

**Document ID:** MI-EY-VAL-001  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Draft — not reviewed or approved  
**Audience:** Movement instructors and early-childhood educators  
**Data policy:** Fictional scenario only; no real child data is collected or stored.

## 1. Purpose

Demonstrate how an instructor might trace one learning intention through the proposed MI-EY OS content model. This is a design test of the documentation structure, not a validated developmental assessment or an approved lesson.

## 2. Fictional use case

An instructor is preparing a short, play-based movement session for a mixed-readiness group in the 2–4 age range. The room has a clear, non-slip area, soft floor markers, lightweight soft balls, and a low, stable line or tape marker. No names, birth dates, photos, medical details, or individual performance records are collected.

## 3. Proposed competency examples

These examples are illustrative labels for testing the content model. They are not normative milestones or validated assessment criteria.

### MI-COMP-LOC-0001 — Moving from place to place
- **Domain:** Locomotor movement
- **Draft intention:** Explore walking and changing direction in a clear space.
- **Observable cue:** Participant moves between two visible markers in a self-selected way, with or without support appropriate to the setting.
- **Possible adaptations:** Increase space; reduce distance; use a visual or verbal cue; allow an alternate movement strategy.
- **Avoid inferring:** Do not label a child as delayed or deficient from one activity or one attempt.

### MI-COMP-STA-0001 — Maintaining and changing body position
- **Domain:** Stability
- **Draft intention:** Explore stopping, starting, and changing body position at a comfortable pace.
- **Observable cue:** Participant attempts to pause or change position in response to a cue; record what happened, not a judgment about the child.
- **Possible adaptations:** Slow the cue; widen the movement area; allow a hand support or seated alternative where suitable.
- **Avoid inferring:** Do not equate a single loss of balance with a stable trait or diagnosis.

### MI-COMP-OBJ-0001 — Interacting with an object
- **Domain:** Object manipulation
- **Draft intention:** Explore rolling or gently sending a soft ball toward a large target.
- **Observable cue:** Participant rolls, pushes, or releases the ball toward the target using their chosen strategy.
- **Possible adaptations:** Move the target closer; use a larger/lighter ball; allow a partner or adult to model the action.
- **Avoid inferring:** Do not rank children by accuracy or compare performance as a measure of worth.

## 4. Proposed movement experience

**ID:** MI-MOVE-VAL-0001  
**Title:** Marker Path and Soft-Ball Explore  
**Status:** Draft example

- **Intention:** Offer low-pressure opportunities to explore locomotor movement, stability, and object interaction.
- **Setup:** Place widely spaced floor markers and a large target. Remove trip hazards; keep the route short and uncluttered.
- **Sequence:** (1) Invite participants to move between markers at a self-selected pace. (2) Invite a stop/start or change-of-direction game. (3) Offer soft balls to roll toward the target. Participation in any part is optional.
- **Equipment:** Flat floor markers, lightweight soft balls, large target.
- **Safety checks:** Inspect the floor and equipment; maintain spacing and active supervision; prevent throwing toward faces; stop if the area becomes crowded or unsafe.
- **Adaptation:** Shorten distance, simplify cues, change target size or distance, provide a seated alternative, or observe rather than participate.
- **Observation:** Record only general activity-level notes needed to improve instruction. Do not attach observations to identifiable children in this public repository.

## 5. Proposed lesson link

**ID:** MI-LESSON-VAL-0001  
**Status:** Draft example

- **Learning intention:** Explore moving through space, changing body position, and sending an object toward a target.
- **Linked competencies:** MI-COMP-LOC-0001; MI-COMP-STA-0001; MI-COMP-OBJ-0001.
- **Linked experience:** MI-MOVE-VAL-0001.
- **Instructor role:** Demonstrate options, supervise continuously, adapt to context, and avoid pressuring a child to perform.
- **Reflection prompts:** Were instructions clear? Could participants choose a suitable challenge? Were space and equipment safe? What would the instructor change next time?

No fixed duration, repetition count, or pass/fail threshold is specified here. These must be adapted to the real setting and reviewed by qualified educators before operational use.

## 7. What this test reveals

1. The proposed content model can link competencies, a movement experience, and a lesson with stable IDs.
2. The model should retain explicit fields for adaptations, safety, observable cues, and review status.
3. Before field testing, instructors should decide which fields are mandatory and whether the activity sequence is practical.
4. This example does not establish age norms, effectiveness, or readiness for production.
