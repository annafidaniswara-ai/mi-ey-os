# MI-EY OS — Quality Review Checklist

**Status: Draft — awaiting Product Owner review.**
Use this checklist before marking a framework, lesson, or movement experience ready for operational use.

## A. Purpose and alignment
- [ ] Intended age/readiness context and learning intention are explicit.
- [ ] Links to competency IDs are valid.
- [ ] Instructions are understandable to the intended instructor.
- [ ] The activity supports exploration and avoids unnecessary comparison.

## B. Evidence quality
- [ ] Research claims have traceable references.
- [ ] Evidence is distinguished from interpretation and design choices.
- [ ] Source population, context, and limitations are considered where material.
- [ ] No citation, study result, validation result, or developmental norm has been invented.
- [ ] Uncertain claims are labeled or omitted.

## C. Safety
- [ ] Space, surface, equipment, group size, and supervision needs are stated.
- [ ] Likely hazards and mitigations are identified.
- [ ] Stop/modify conditions are clear.
- [ ] Intensity and task complexity can be adapted to the participant and context.
- [ ] The content does not imply that one activity or observation can diagnose a child.

## D. Inclusion and adaptation
- [ ] Alternatives exist for different abilities, confidence levels, and equipment access.
- [ ] Instructions avoid shaming, coercion, and deficit labels.
- [ ] Participation can be adapted without stigmatizing a child.
- [ ] Age bands are planning context, not rigid universal expectations.

## E. Observation and privacy
- [ ] Observation cues describe visible behavior rather than assumed traits.
- [ ] Examples are fictional or anonymized.
- [ ] No unnecessary child-identifying data is included.
- [ ] Real child-related data requires a separate access, consent, retention, and security review.

## F. Document and repository integrity
- [ ] File path, ID, version, and status are present.
- [ ] Internal links and referenced IDs resolve.
- [ ] Draft / In review / Approved status is accurate.
- [ ] No credentials, tokens, private keys, or secrets are present.
- [ ] Change is reviewable and has a meaningful commit message.

## G. Approval record
- [ ] Reviewer role/name is recorded according to privacy rules.
- [ ] Unresolved issues are listed.
- [ ] Product Owner approval is explicit where required.
- [ ] A commit is not treated as approval.

## Review outcomes
- **Draft:** content is being developed.
- **Needs revision:** one or more required checks fail.
- **In review:** checks are complete and content is submitted for review.
- **Approved:** an authorized human reviewer explicitly accepted it.
- **Rejected / Superseded:** rationale and replacement are recorded.

## Minimum acceptance rule
Any unresolved critical safety, privacy, evidence-integrity, or scope issue blocks approval. This checklist supports review but does not replace qualified judgment or local safety procedures.
