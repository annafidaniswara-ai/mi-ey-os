# MI-EY OS Curriculum

**Status: Draft — sequence not yet approved.**

## Scope

Organize competencies and movement experiences into a coherent progression for the intended platform age range of 2–6 years.

## First bounded deliverable

Develop and review the Developmental Competency Framework for ages 2–4 first. Do not infer that a complete curriculum for ages 2–6 exists yet.

## Planned outputs

- Curriculum map and domain relationships.
- Suggested sequencing and progression logic.
- Adaptation pathways and instructor decision points.
- Links to lessons and movement experiences.
- Review and evaluation plan.

## Dependencies

Approved vision and principles, reviewed competency framework, lesson model, and instructor feedback.
